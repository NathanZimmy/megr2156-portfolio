# About Me
## My name is Nathaneal Zimmerman
I am a mechanical engineering major at the University of North Carolina at Charlotte. I am pursuing a concentration in motorsports as well as a minor in mathematics. I chose mechanical engineering for a multitude of reasons, but the simplest one was that I didn't want to stop at just being a mechanic. I have always had a love and passion for cars or really anything with a motor, but I was also really good in school growing up. I made straight A's, took college classes in high school, and never really had to study. So when the time came around to decide, I thought to myself, why cut myself short at being a mechanic when I could go bigger and learn how to design the things I love working on. So I decided to apply to UNC Charlotte's engineering program because they are the only one of two universities that has a motorsports curriculum, and they are a local school to me. From then on, I've come to love learning about engineering and everything it has to offer, and I'm really excited to continue to learn how to start designing my own ideas and what it really means to be an engineer.
